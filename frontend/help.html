Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Select a product, click Add to Cart, review your cart, and proceed to checkout.

How do I view my orders?

Open the Orders page from the navigation bar to view your purchase history.

Can I remove products from my cart?

Yes. Open your cart and click the Remove button beside any item.

Is my login secure?

Yes. User authentication is handled using Amazon Cognito.
